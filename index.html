overbid.in

India's pay-to-rank leaderboard. Brands pay in rupees by UPI to outbid each other for #1, and the weekly board resets every Monday.

  • A live board that updates for everyone the moment a bid lands
  • UPI checkout with payment webhooks, refunds and disputes handled
  • A search-friendly page for every brand and category
  • Admin tools for reviews, logos, free listings and traffic

ThreadFit for Reddit

A Chrome extension that finds Reddit threads where your product genuinely helps, scores each for buying intent, and drafts an honest reply you post yourself.

  • A side panel that reads Reddit from your own tab, never posting for you
  • An intent score that explains why each thread fits
  • AI drafts with your own key, falling back across Gemini, Groq and Claude
  • No server: everything stays in the browser
